Tomasz Kobialka (b. 1983 Mielec, Poland)

Tomasz Kobialka is a Polish-Australian artist and Software Engineer who lives and works in London.

Kobialka’s artistic practice examines the ideologies and power structures through which financial and economic systems shape everyday life. Working across digital media, sculpture, and installation, the work often draws on personal experiences and narratives.
